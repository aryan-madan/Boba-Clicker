**Boba Clicker** is a simple ‘cookie-clicker-esque’ idle game I made for Boba Bash Delhi! You click a cup of Boba to earn more Boba and get upgrades to get more Boba!

## Upgrades

| Upgrade | Description | Cost | Per Click | Per Second |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Extra Pearls | +1 per click | 15 | +1 | — |
| Bigger Straw | +3 per click | 100 | +3 | — |
| Slack Help | Hack Clubbers in #boba-bash | 500 | — | +8 |
| Workshop | Everyone learns flexbox | 2,000 | — | +25 |
| Boba Bash | A whole city shows up | 10,000 | — | +100 |
